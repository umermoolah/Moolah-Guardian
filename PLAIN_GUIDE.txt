Moolah Guardian — Plain English Guide

Quick summary
- Name: Moolah Guardian
- Purpose: Lets parents see and control certain things on a child’s phone or tablet (apps, websites, location, messages, and subscriptions).
- Who it's for: Parents or guardians who want a single place to check device health, activity, and manage rules and subscriptions.

How the app works (simple steps)
1. Install and open the app, then sign up or sign in. (Server: /login or /register)
2. Link (pair) your child’s device by scanning a QR or entering a code and confirming identity. (Server: /parentAppSecurityCheck → /parentKidDeviceConnect)
3. After linking, the child’s device appears on your dashboard (a list of children/devices). (Server: /getConnectedKidDevices)
4. Tap a child to see details: battery, last active time, location, apps, messages, and controls. (Server: /getSingleDeviceDetail, /getDeviceThreadsEncryption, /getThreadMessagesEncryption, /getDeviceLocation)
5. When you take an action (block app, request location), the app sends that command; it’s applied immediately if the child device is online, or applied when the device next connects. (Server: /sendActionToDevice)

Main features — plain English (and the simple backend name)
- Account & Login: Sign up and sign in so you can use the app. (Server: /login, /register — OTP: /send-mobile-otp, /verifyMobileOtp)
- Pair a child’s device: Connect a child’s phone/tablet to your account. (Server: /parentAppSecurityCheck, /parentKidDeviceConnect)
- Dashboard: See all connected children with quick info like battery and last active time. (Server: /getConnectedKidDevices)
- Device details: View one child’s device info, apps, messages, and location. (Server: /getSingleDeviceDetail, /getDeviceThreadsEncryption, /getThreadMessagesEncryption, /getDeviceLocation)
- Location: See the child’s last known location on a map or as an address/time. (Server: /getDeviceLocation)
- App usage: See which apps the child uses and how long. (Server: /getAppUsage)
- Block apps: Block an app on the child device so it can’t be opened (takes effect when device is online). (Server: /blacklistApp, /getBlacklistedApps)
- Block websites: Add websites to a blocked list so they can’t be visited. (Server: /getBlockedUrlsEncryption, /addBlockedUrl)
- Messages & chat: View chat threads and messages if the child device shares them. (Server: /getDeviceThreadsEncryption, /getThreadMessagesEncryption)
- SMS monitoring status: Check if SMS monitoring is on and enable if allowed. (Server: /getMSMSMonitoringStatus, /enableMSMSMonitoring)
- Remote actions: Send commands to the child device (like request location or lock). (Server: /sendActionToDevice)
- Wallet & subscriptions: Buy a subscription or check wallet status for premium features. (Server: /createPaymentIntent, /getWalletEnabled, /enableWallet)
- Ads: Small ads may appear in the app if you’re not subscribed. (Server: /getBannerAds)
- Feedback & support: Send feedback or a review to the app team. (Server: /feedback, /submitReview)

Simple data flow — plain words
- The child device sends allowed info (like app usage and location) to the service.
- Your parent app asks the service for that info and shows it to you.
- When you press a control, your app tells the service and the service tells the child device.
- If the child device is offline, the change takes effect when it reconnects.

Common problems and quick fixes
- Data is old: The child’s phone might be off or not connected. Ask them to open the app or check internet.
- Block didn’t work: The child’s device must be online and have necessary permissions. Check the command status and try again.
- Location not updating: Make sure location is enabled on the child’s device.
- Payment failed: Check payment details and retry; contact support if it keeps failing.

Privacy & legal reminder
- This app shows personal information from another person’s device. Only use it on devices you own or have explicit permission to monitor (for example, your child’s device).
- Laws about monitoring devices and messages vary — make sure you follow local rules.
